Original physical vehicle: 洋流號

The real campaign vehicle is credited collectively to the Shen Po-yang campaign team / 沈伯洋競選團隊. Official campaign project · September 30 launch report identifying the campaign team · October 25 report showing the later transparent enclosure.

Its ship-themed pickup, deck, helm, mast, flags, speakers and later enclosure informed this LEGO interpretation. CL Kao adapted those visual features using the separately credited upstream pickup assembly; LEGO construction, proportions, attachment details, instructions and the DSS name are project adaptations. The original physical vehicle design is not claimed as our creation.

The individual vehicle designer and fabricator have not been independently identified. The collective campaign credit does not establish those roles; poster and graphic-design credits are separate. The original physical design is excluded from our CC BY-SA grant. Reference record and verification status.

New enclosure and speech-bubble scene

CL Kao added the transparent enclosure, relocated the helm and outboard rails, and planned the revised installation. Philippe Hurbain supplied the 59349 clear panel and 62711 bob-hair meshes; Steve Bliss supplied the plain 973 torso and 30374 bar meshes. The 小英 figure uses an adapted catalog-parts pose, a custom jacket graphic and one real 2335 flag with two speech-bubble decals, 叫你休息 and 講不聽. These custom graphics were drawn for this project; no external portrait was incorporated. The table below maps every active part to its original author.

Meme inspiration: AYA

The 小英拍背／叫你休息 meme is credited to AYA／AYA全糖腐女中心 (@theaya1108). Original-comic link reported by 放言 · source and attribution · report crediting AYA for the 叫你休息 comic.

This project adapts the meme into one two-sided LEGO speech-bubble banner. CL Kao drew the new decal layout; the faces read「叫你休息」and「講不聽」.「講不聽」is our adaptation of the dialogue, not a claim to quote the original comic exactly.
